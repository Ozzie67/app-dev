## Overview

**Attack on Titan** (*Shingeki no Kyojin*) is a Japanese anime series based on the manga by **Hajime Isayama**. It follows Eren Yeager and his friends as they fight to survive against giant humanoid creatures called Titans.

> "If you win, you live. If you lose, you die. If you don't fight, you can't win!"
> — Eren Yeager

---

## Why I Love It

- Amazing storytelling and plot twists
- Deep and complex characters
- Epic soundtrack and animation
- Themes of freedom, war, and sacrifice

## My Favorite Character
